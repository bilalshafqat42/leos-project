Pin + Scrub section pins in viewport, animation is scroll-position driven
Fade-up element enters on scroll with opacity + upward translate
Stagger group of elements animate in sequence, offset in time
Clip-path reveal image/panel reveals via animated mask, not fade
Parallax background/image moves at a different rate than scroll
Count-up number animates from 0 to final value on entry

What this document is

Structural and motion wireframe for the four LEOS pages, showing section order, layout intent and the on-scroll behaviour for each block. Layout patterns are adapted from the two reference walkthroughs you shared (springs.estate and Elyse Residence) — full-bleed photography, one giant serif word per moment, pinned numbered sequences, animated stat counters — rebuilt for a construction/renovation business in LEOS's own black / gold / cream palette, not the reference sites' colours. Boxes below are structural placeholders, not final visuals; the real styling already exists in the codebase.
